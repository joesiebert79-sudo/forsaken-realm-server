Forsaken Realm v0.90 - Base and Trader Expansion

Five existing home styles each support eight separate base levels.
Upgrades use progressively stronger materials, local storage and an upgraded construction bench.
Walls, gates, walkways and four individually built corner towers support server-validated elevated combat.
Eight base outbuildings include functional greenhouse, workshop, cookhouse, smokehouse, stable, husbandry, storage and trophy interiors.
Home storage and a chosen workstation can be built at indoor snap points.
Both named trader hubs remain neutral and keep their established world positions.
Traders combine copper, silver and gold and make exact change: 10 copper = 1 silver; 10 silver = 1 gold.
Legendary, Mythic and Unique equipment is restricted to flash deals, dangerous exploration and bosses.
Six approved classes and seven approved companions remain selectable.
The map, player progress and player codes are preserved. This archive contains no player saves, codes or signing keys.
The bundled Android installer is offered by the server's local /phone page.

Validation: 89 server tests, menu navigation, 1,200 character poses, 700 companion poses, item/atlas checks, actual ZIP installation and local phone download checks passed.
Physical-phone performance has not been measured in this workspace.
Some original item artwork is retained and rendered at the shared 48-pixel scale where the approved atlas lacks the item.
