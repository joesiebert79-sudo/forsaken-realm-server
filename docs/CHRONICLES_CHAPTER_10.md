# Forsaken Realm Chronicles
## Book One — The Age of Splendor
### Chapter Ten — The King's Last Confession

**Status:** Approved by author October 31, 2026. This is the spoken condensed version approved in the conversation; the complete long-form Chapter Ten draft is preserved in that conversation and should be reconciled into this manuscript before final publication.

Aldric stood before the royal observatory doors with the iron key, remembering his father's warning that everything he taught might be a lie.

Inside, the chair his father died in stood untouched. His journal said the seven spires were warnings, not monuments, and that kingdoms survived by surrendering whole memories.

Seraphine couldn't remember a village she swore she'd visited.

At Ravenshollow, a hidden city stirred beneath the graves, and a bell sounded underground.

Aldric found his father's signet ring on a creature that claimed to be a messenger of those who remember. He discovered a silver chain hidden beneath the floor, and when he raised it, the creature screamed, "The sixth bloodline must remain bound."

The observatory went silent except for seven slow bell strikes as Aldric swore to go to Ravenshollow.

**End of Chapter Ten — The King's Last Confession**
