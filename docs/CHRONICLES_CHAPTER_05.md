# Forsaken Realm Chronicles
## Book One — The Age of Splendor
### Chapter Five — The Echoing Names

The bells kept ringing long after their sound died.

By morning, every well in Ravenshollow whispered when approached. The traveler walked past a bucket and heard his own name said inside his skull, not in his voice, not in any voice he recognized.

Children drew circles in ash and left one space empty. The clerk refused to drink, tongue blistering with half-formed names each time he tried.

Seraphine rode south with the treaty pressed to her heart. At each spire she passed, blue light bent toward her saddle as if listening. She began remembering things she had never lived: a coronation she hadn't attended, a promise spoken in a language with no living mouth.

Aldric waited in an abandoned chapel. When Seraphine arrived, he didn't greet her by title.

“Tell me what you remember,” he said.

She told him.

When she finished, both knew that whatever the six had buried was coming to collect interest.
