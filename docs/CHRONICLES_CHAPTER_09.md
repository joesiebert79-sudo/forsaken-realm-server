# Forsaken Realm Chronicles
## Book One — The Age of Splendor
### Chapter Nine — The Sins of Our Fathers

**Status:** Approved by author on October 31, 2026.

Aldric had heard his father's voice in dreams, but never laughing like this.

Seraphine warned him, "That's not your father."

"Then why does he know my name?" Aldric asked.

Beneath the seventh throne, something chuckled, and the chamber felt suddenly occupied by seven shadows. One voice spoke with tenderness, another with hunger.

Far south, the Book of Oaths wrote by itself, ink moving as if guided by an unseen hand. Brenn told them the six kings chained their bloodlines to the bargain.

"When truth returns," he said, "the six bloodlines must answer."

East of Thornhaven, a soldier smiled beneath the seventh bell and whispered, "The king remembers," before the bell rang with no clapper at all.

By the time night fell, a raven delivered seven words scrawled in blood:

**THE FIRST KING HAS ALREADY BEEN JUDGED.**

Aldric heard the echo in his bones and, for the first time since kneeling beside his father's grave, he wasn't afraid. He was furious.

"Then I'll open every door you buried," he said, and the seventh throne shuddered in answer.
