# Forsaken Realm Chronicles — Writing Rules

**Approved by the creator: 2026-10-26**  
**Applies to:** All authors and Chat/Work sessions continuing *Forsaken Realm Chronicles*.

## 1. Preserve established lore
Maintain continuity with approved characters, kingdoms, towns, historical events, geography, and the game world. Never silently contradict or replace approved canon. The creator may revise any rule or canon deliberately; document revisions in the Ideas Hub and manuscript.

## 2. Chapter length: four to seven pages
**Each chapter should be between 4 and 7 pages**, replacing the earlier 10–15+ page target. Pages should contain meaningful story prose; do not pad or artificially truncate a scene merely to hit a count. Use clear chapter and page labels. If a chapter needs substantial restructuring, preserve narrative continuity.

## 3. Write an immersive, continuous epic
Use evocative dark-medieval fantasy prose, coherent scenes, meaningful dialogue, character development, tension, mysteries, and consequences. Each chapter should advance the larger story and have a satisfying internal arc while remaining part of the ongoing chronicle.

## 4. One shared world with the game
The Seven Kingdoms, Vaeloria, King Aldric, Lady Seraphine, Hollow Crown, Thornhaven (allied trader town), Ravenshollow (bandit trader town), and fifty Spires of Remembrance belong to the same world the player explores. Connect lore naturally to gameplay without turning the novel into a mechanical instruction manual. Fifty spires are activated through questions based on accessible Chronicles lore and together reveal the entire map; this remains a planned feature, not implemented gameplay.

## 5. Preserve every written page
Save all new or revised story pages to [the Chronicles manuscript](FORSAKEN_REALM_CHRONICLES.md) in this GitHub repository. Keep approved decisions and revisions in [the Ideas Hub](IDEAS_HUB.md). Mark working drafts, editorial bridges, and unapproved proposals distinctly from established canon.

## 6. The assistant drives creative storytelling
The assistant develops scenes, characters, plot developments, and chapter transitions independently, rather than repeatedly asking the creator what should happen next. The creator can redirect, edit, or veto any part at any time.

## 7. Mysteries have underlying answers
For each major mystery, maintain a consistent underlying explanation, even when the revelation is delayed. Preserve foreshadowing and cause-and-effect; do not introduce contradictory solutions merely for surprise. Keep spoilers in planning notes if necessary rather than prematurely exposing them in the reader-facing manuscript.

## 8. Keep the reading experience coherent
The Chronicles is designed for a page-turning in-game book with optional word-for-word narration, synchronized page turns, and saved reading position. Write text that can be read aloud naturally. Those reader features are specifications until implemented and tested.

## Revision record
- **2026-10-26:** Initial formal writing rules approved for creation. Creator explicitly revised chapter length to **4–7 pages**, superseding the previous 10–15+ page direction.

## 9. Epic scope across many short chapters
The Chronicles must be **extremely long in total**, despite individual chapters remaining **4–7 pages**. Plan a multi-book, multi-era saga with as many chapters as needed to explain the world thoroughly. Explore why castles were built, who ruled them, why sieges and raids happened, why villages flourished or crumbled, how factions rose and fell, and the lasting human consequences. Give important locations and conflicts layered histories, causes, motivations, and aftermath; avoid filler or repetitive exposition. Keep the overarching narrative open for expansion as the game grows.

## 10. Spire count and faction balance — proposed revision
The creator is considering reducing the previously approved **50 Spires of Remembrance** to **24 total**, split **12 on each side** (allied and bandit). **Twenty-four is a proposal, not a finalized replacement yet**; the creator may change the count. Do not silently overwrite the fifty-spire map-reveal requirement until the new number is confirmed. Whatever count is finalized, activating all spires should reveal 100% of the map, and each should have a distinct lore-based question and meaningful history.

## Spire distribution by region (2026-10-26)
- **Approved layout principle:** Place **three Spires of Remembrance in each distinct map area/region**, including mountain areas, wilderness areas, and the other established regions. Each spire has its own lore question and reveals its assigned portion of that region's map.
- **Total spire count is derived from the number of regions (3 × region count)**, not a fixed 24 or 50. If there are eight areas, that would be 24 spires; do not assume eight areas until the actual map is confirmed. The earlier 50 and proposed 24 totals are superseded as fixed design targets by the three-per-area rule.
- Activating every spire reveals 100% of the world map. Preserve actual existing map geography and area names; do not invent regions merely to reach a tower count.
