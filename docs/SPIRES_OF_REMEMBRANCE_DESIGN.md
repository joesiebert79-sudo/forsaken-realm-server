Status: **Design approved; not implemented.** Creator selected the second dark-gothic set and approved a game-camera adaptation. ## Twelve spire visual archetypes
1. Ancient — weathered gothic stone, blue runic light.
2. Elemental — raw elemental energy, orange flame.
3. Shadow — black jagged masonry, violet magic.
4. Celestial — pale stone, radiant gold halo.
5. War — martial banners, crimson illumination.
6. Wilderness — reclaimed stone, green nature glow.
7. Mountain — stone and ice, cold blue light.
8. Desert — sand-worn sandstone, amber energy.
9. Coastal — sea-worn stone, blue tidal magic.
10. Forest — vine-wrapped tower, bright green magic.
11. Ruined — shattered tower, white spectral awakening.
12. Eternal — floating fragments, deep-blue arcane rings.

Each type has **dormant** and **activated** visual states. Concept art depicts 24 state variations; these are **reference images, not production-ready game assets**. The existing 3-spires-per-map-region rule still applies: **twelve types are a visual catalog, not a new count of towers per region**.

## Approved multiplayer progression model
- Every player must personally discover and complete the lore challenge at a spire to unlock its map-reveal reward.
- A spire awakened by one player becomes a visible, glowing landmark for everyone in the shared world; this does **not** unlock other players' map progress or skip their challenges.
- Nearby players can witness a brief first-awakening event (skyward beam, runes, tremor).
- Record personal spire completion and nonrepeating lore-question history separately from global shared-world visual state.
- Each region has exactly three spires; all completed reveal the entire world map under the established rules.
- Implementation is deferred to the Work session; do not modify game code here.
