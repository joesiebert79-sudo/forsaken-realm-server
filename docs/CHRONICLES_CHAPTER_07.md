Chapter Seven — The Bells Below

At sunset, the ground folded inward like breath under one grave. Old Brenn watched, hands pressed to glass. Others stayed indoors, but the traveler went anyway. Seven stones stood dry in the rain, each bare of name, each carved with a crown. The clerk followed, shaking.

"We shouldn't be here."

"We're already late," the traveler said.

The nearest grave spoke: "We kept the oath. They buried us for it."

Six more tremors answered, then three knocks from inside the seventh. A child's voice threaded the wet air: "The last witnesses."

The clerk clutched his coat. "Witnesses to what?"

"To the vow they refuse to remember," the traveler said.

Far below, the forgotten king heard their names and whispered, "Do not come yet."

Around him, chains warmed with old guilt, and something listened for the first bell they could not hear.
